## Specification requirements

- CORE-002 GeometryTolerance remains the distance/angle/snap threshold
- MESH-004 / MESH-OP-007 triangulation winding and FaceId mapping stay deterministic
- Dependency policy: `robust-predicates` behind `GeometryPredicates` in `@modeling-kit/math`

## Goal

Wrap `orient2d` and `orient3d` from `robust-predicates` as a small internal geometry-predicate service. Use it for orientation *signs* in projected polygon winding, ear clipping, segment intersection, collinearity/coplanarity, and knife/split classification. Do not replace distance, snapping, or user-tolerance comparisons.

## Dependencies permitted

- `robust-predicates` 3.0.3 in `@modeling-kit/math` only
